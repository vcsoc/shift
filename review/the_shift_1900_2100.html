What does the crossover mean?

Both lines are conceptual indices from 0 to 100, not measured historical series. They represent different constructs and share a visual scale only for storytelling. Their crossing is not a mathematically meaningful threshold at which AI stops needing people. Historical milestone dates are grounded in recognizable developments, while all plotted values are illustrative. Years after 2026 are hypothetical scenarios. Human oversight, responsibility and control are social and governance choices, not automatic consequences of technical progress.
